the process

From Swings to Dreams.

You never know when a new song idea is going to spring up — it could be sitting on a porch swing strumming my guitar, or waking up at 2 a.m. with a new melody in my mind. Most of these started out as voice memos on a phone or lyrics hastily scribbled in the dark. Fingerpicked and rhythmic, shaped by whatever memories the day left behind. The studio is upstairs. The inspiration is everywhere else.
